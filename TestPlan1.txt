    Jest->>Mocks: Initialize global mocks (logger, axios, config)
    Mocks-->>Jest: Mocks ready

        Jest->>MongoDB: Clear collections (afterEach)

        Jest->>Jest: Verify coverage thresholds (80%)
    end
    
    Jest->>MongoDB: Close database connection
